It all started with one kettle

The story of Kettle & Crumb

Kettle & Crumb began as a single kettle, a small oven, and a simple idea: a cup of tea should never be rushed. We started by serving neighbours and friends, and the word spread one cup at a time.

Today we still brew our tea slowly and bake every morning, from warm samosas to cakes and butter cookies. Nothing comes out of a packet.

Whether you stop by for a quick cup, bring your laptop for an hour, or order for a whole office, we want you to leave feeling looked after.
